CardoMax · Jul–Sep 2026 · 179 ads

Liquid energy and hydration sticks · US

Energy you can see

CardoMax sells liquid energy and hydration in a stick. The client's brief: make people thirsty, make the liquid obvious, and keep it bright and young. More Red Bull, less lab.

I split the account into lanes, each with its own visual language: a gaming lane written in game words (XP Boost, Legendary Item), offer ads built around the free 8-stick pack, and bright liquid shots for the gym, the jobsite and the commute.

Lanes
Gaming · Offer · Gym · Jobsite · Hydration

Sweet Bamboo · FW26 photo library · 450+ images

Kidswear · lifestyle photo library

A fall collection, before it arrived

The fall collection was still on a boat and the ad account was paused. Sweet Bamboo needed a full lifestyle library before the clothes even landed.

I turned flat print swatches into photos of children wearing them, with strict rules: long sleeves for fall, trims that match the body colour, hidden zippers, prints at their real scale. For the casting I wrote 102 different children's faces so the library never repeats itself.

Mānuka Health · Sep–Oct 2026 · DE + AT

Mānuka honey · German market

Premium, in German

Mānuka Health sells New Zealand honey in Germany and Austria. Every ad is in German, and every jar has to read right: the MGO grade, the weight, the label.

I generate the scenes, then check each label against the real jar. On a honey jar a wrong number is a wrong claim, so nothing ships until the label matches.

Kai Fine · Jul–Aug 2026

Fine jewellery · US

The jewellery stays the hero

The account's best ad named a colour in a serif headline and brought back 8.22 times its cost in 30 days. My brief was to carry that format to new stones without changing one word of the copy.

The client flagged one ad: the fig was stealing attention. I made the earrings bigger, lower and centred, pushed the fig back out of focus, and added one line to the prompt so the jewellery always stays the hero. The round took less than a day.

Brief
8 ads × 2 sizes, copy locked
Stones
Emerald · amethyst · turquoise
Rule
Copy the real piece exactly, never invent it

Process · the same loop for every brand

How I work

Several brands every week. The brief changes, the way of working doesn't.

  1. 01Read the briefWhat we sell, to who, in which sizes.
  2. 02Research the brandColours, fonts, voice and the ads that already won.
  3. 03Gather referencesReal product shots and winning ads.
  4. 04Write the promptCamera, light, set and casting.
  5. 05Generate and pickRuns, rerolls and quality checks.
  6. 06Finish in Figma and PhotoshopLive type, cutouts and retouching.
  7. 07Deliver and iterateNamed files in the right slots, then client feedback.

One designer · a whole crew

One ad used to need a whole team

On a real shoot every one of these is a separate job. With AI I do all of them, and to do them well I learned each job's own words. The purple terms come straight from my prompts.

The photographer

I set the camera, the lens, the light and the angle in the prompt, like planning a real shoot.

85mm · f/2.2 · golden hour · rim light
The casting agent

I decide who is in the ad: age, build, style. For Sweet Bamboo I wrote 102 children's faces.

real build · salt and pepper · mid 40s
The set designer

Locations, props and backgrounds are all written by me: a freezer, an elevator, a Texas backyard.

limewash · terracotta · brushed metal
The stylist

Wardrobe and product placement: what people wear, how the product sits, what is in their hand.

raglan sleeve · patch pocket · tapered
The art director

The idea itself: the concept, the references and the rules that keep a batch looking like one campaign.

moodboard · hierarchy · thumb stop
The typesetter

Every word is placed by me in Figma: font, size, colour and position, always inside the safe zones.

letter spacing · line height · scrim
The retoucher

Photoshop fixes by hand: warped rings redrawn, reflections repainted, labels rebuilt.

layer mask · healing brush · cutout
The producer

Cost checks before every run, deadlines met, every file named right and delivered to the right slot.

dry run · delivery slot · 1:1 · 9:16

Craft · drag the handle

Raw image to finished ad

The model gives me a starting image. The cutout, the type, the retouching and the layout are my part.
